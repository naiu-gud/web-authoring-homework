Our story

A friendly place where curiosity can grow.

Greenfield Coding Club is a welcoming community for people who want to learn web design by making real things together.

We believe beginners learn best when the atmosphere feels calm, supportive, and creative rather than stressful or overly technical.

Our purpose

Our mission

Our goal is to help every member build one useful web page at a time while understanding what each piece of HTML and CSS actually does.

Questions are always welcome, experiments are encouraged, and nobody has to figure everything out alone.

Learn together

We explain concepts in plain language and make time to practice them slowly.

Build with care

We value readable code, thoughtful design, and websites that feel pleasant to use.

Share progress

Small improvements matter, and every finished experiment is something worth celebrating.

What matters to us

Learn, make, share.

There is room here for complete beginners and experienced makers alike.

Bring a question, a small idea, or a page you want to improve. The goal is not perfection — it is to understand a little more each time.
